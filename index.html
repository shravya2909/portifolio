About

How I got here

Hi, I'm Shravya. I'm the one who shows up to a mess and, a few weeks later, leaves a system in its place. Order from chaos is the part of this work I love most.

I started in machine learning at Spydx, learning how ML concepts get applied to real projects. Then I moved into SAP as an SAP analyst intern, learning how it's used, why it's sold, and the business case behind it.

At Ktern, KaarTech's AI product arm, I worked with enterprise clients on where AI agents could take manual effort off a team's plate. Now, as Project Coordinator at KaarTech, I own delivery and the client relationship across three UK & Ireland accounts: BAE Systems, Ramsay Health Care and Life Health Foods.

It wasn't a random walk. Each stage gave me the skill for the next, and it points at project, product or engineering management. Outside work I'm a jack-of-all-trades: music, writing, photography, DIY and design. Chennai born and raised, and it shows.
