About

How I got here

Hi — I'm Shravya. If you asked the people I've worked with what I'm actually good at, most of them wouldn't say "coding" or "project plans." They'd say I'm the one who shows up to a mess and, a few weeks later, there's a system where the mess used to be — watching order emerge from chaos, and knowing I'm the reason it did.

It didn't start that way. I started in machine learning, interning at Spydx, where I spent most of my time learning and observing how ML and deep learning concepts actually get applied to real-world projects — not client-facing yet, not owning a deployed solution, just paying close attention to how the theory turned into something usable. It was my first real look at the gap between knowing a concept and knowing how it gets used.

From there I went a layer deeper into the enterprise side, as an SAP analyst intern — learning how SAP systems are actually used, why they're sold to customers in the first place, and the business case underneath most SAP engagements. It wasn't hands-on technical work yet, but it's why I can now sit in a room where SAP comes up and genuinely follow the conversation instead of just nodding along.

That grounding is what let the next step click. As a Customer Success Engineer in AI Automation at Ktern — KaarTech's AI product arm — I worked directly with enterprise clients figuring out where AI agents could realistically take manual effort off a team's plate — every opportunity had to answer two questions at once: can we build this, and does it move the needle on margin and ROI for the client. That's where the technical and client-facing sides of me finally started talking to each other.

Today, as a Project Coordinator at KaarTech, it comes together. I coordinate across three client accounts in the UK & Ireland region — BAE Systems and Ramsay Health Care on AMS/support, and Life Health Foods on an ECC-to-S/4HANA transformation — and I own delivery, resourcing, and the client relationship across all of it. Accountability doesn't get to be selective: it's not just claiming the wins, it's owning the misses too.

None of this was a random walk. ML taught me to scope an ambiguous problem. SAP taught me how enterprise systems actually work. Customer success taught me to connect technical possibility to business value. Coordination taught me to hold all of it — timelines, people, budgets, trust — at once. Each stage handed me the skill I needed for the next, and it's pointing somewhere specific: my next step in project, product, or engineering management, where I get to do all of this on purpose.

Outside of client calls and status decks, I'm genuinely a jack-of-all-trades — music, writing, photography, DIY projects, a running interest in interior and fashion design. Same instinct as the day job: taking something with too many moving parts and finding the version with taste and order. Chennai born and raised, and it shows.
